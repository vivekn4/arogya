Soothe that scratchy throat naturally

By the Arogya wellness team · Updated October 2026 · 4 min read

That scratchy, swollen feeling when you swallow — sore throats are miserable but usually harmless, often arriving with a cold. These natural remedies soothe the irritation while your body fights it off.

1. Warm salt-water gargle

The classic for a reason. Dissolve half a teaspoon of salt in a glass of warm water and gargle for 30 seconds, several times a day. It draws out excess fluid from inflamed tissues.

2. Honey in warm water or tea

A spoonful of honey in warm water or herbal tea coats and soothes the throat. (Never give honey to children under 1 year old.)

3. Ginger and turmeric tea

Simmer fresh ginger slices with a pinch of turmeric for 10 minutes. Both are natural anti-inflammatories — add honey once it's cool enough to drink.

4. Stay hydrated and humid

Dry air worsens throat pain. Drink plenty of fluids and, if you can, run a humidifier or breathe in steam from a bowl of hot water with a towel over your head.

5. Rest your voice

Talking and shouting strain an already irritated throat. Keep chatter to a minimum for a day or two — whispering actually strains more than soft speaking, so just speak quietly.

6. Warm broths and soft foods

Warm (not hot) soups and broths soothe on the way down and keep you nourished when swallowing hurts. Avoid very hot, spicy, or crunchy foods for now.

When to see a doctor

See a doctor if your sore throat is severe, lasts more than a week, or comes with a high fever, trouble breathing or swallowing, a rash, or swollen glands that don't improve.
